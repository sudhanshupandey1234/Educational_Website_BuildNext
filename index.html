01 · About BuildNext

Built by students, for students.

BuildNext is a student community at Chandigarh University, Uttar Pradesh, Unnao. We learn technology by doing it — together.

◎

Our mission

Learn by doing. Workshops, small projects and peer support that help students pick up real skills in AI, web development and programming.

◐

Our vision

A campus where any student can find people to learn with, build with, and ask questions without feeling out of place.

✦

How we learn

Start where you are. Sessions are hands-on, questions are welcome, and progress means something you built and can explain.

03 · Learning Journey

Five steps, in order.

Start anywhere. Go at your own pace.

  1. 01

    Explore

    Try short sessions and demos to find what interests you.

  2. 02

    Learn

    Build the basics with guided practice and room to ask questions.

  3. 03

    Build

    Make a small project you can finish and care about.

  4. 04

    Collaborate

    Work in a team, review each other’s work, and share the load.

  5. 05

    Showcase

    Present your project, get feedback, and add it to your portfolio.

Going back a step is normal. Foundations are worth revisiting.

06 · Community

Learn with other people.

BuildNext works because students help each other. Here’s how to take part.

◍

Peer learning

Study together, debug together, explain things to each other. Teaching is often the fastest way to learn.

◎

Teamwork, practised

Small teams, clear roles, honest feedback. Plan work, review code, and present together.

✦

Mentorship

Students further along help newer ones — with questions, encouragement and next steps.

Ways to contribute

  • Help run a workshop or study session
  • Mentor a beginner through their first project
  • Review a teammate’s code or design
  • Write up what you learned
  • Help organise an event

Join the community

Open to students at Chandigarh University, Uttar Pradesh — any branch, any year, any starting point.
